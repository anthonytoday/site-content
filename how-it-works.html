How It Works

From payment to working template in about two minutes

No account to create on this site, nothing to download, nothing to install. You pay, you get a link, you click it once, and the template is in your own Notion workspace where you can change anything you like.

1

Pick your template and pay

Every template has its own page with screenshots, a full list of what is inside, and the price. Checkout is handled by Stripe: card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or Link. You will be asked for your email, because that is where the template link is sent.

You do not need a Notion paid plan. Every template works on the free personal plan.

2

Get your access link

The confirmation screen appears immediately after payment, and the same link arrives by email from admin@anthonytoday.com. Keep that email: it is your permanent proof of purchase and your route to future updates.

Nothing in your inbox after a few minutes? Check spam, then reply to any of my emails or write to info@anthonytoday.com and I will send it manually.

3

Click Duplicate

Opening the link shows the template inside Notion, in read-only preview. Press the Duplicate button at the top right. Notion asks which workspace to copy it into, and if you belong to more than one, pick the workspace you actually work in rather than a personal one you will abandon.

The copy is yours. It is not shared with me, I cannot see it, and nothing syncs back. Rename it, delete half of it, restructure it: it is your workspace now.

4

Start with the Read Me

Every template opens with a short Read Me First section at the top. It tells you which databases matter, what to fill in first, and what to ignore until later. Ten minutes there saves an hour of guessing.

Work through one section before touching the rest. The fastest way to abandon a new system is to try to populate all of it on day one.

Common questions

Can I use it on mobile?

Yes. Every template works in the Notion mobile app. Product pages show mobile screenshots so you can see how it behaves on a phone before buying.

What if I already have a Notion workspace with my own pages?

The duplicate lands alongside your existing pages without touching them. You can move it into any section afterwards, or drag pieces out of it and leave the rest.

Do I get updates?

Yes, and they are free. When a template is improved I email the new duplication link. Your existing copy is untouched, so you choose whether to move across.

Can I share it with my team?

Yes, inside your own organisation. Share it the way you share any Notion page. Reselling it or redistributing it publicly is not permitted.

What if it is not right for me?

Reply to the delivery email within 14 days and I refund it, no questions asked.
