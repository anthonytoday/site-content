Instructions

Install Anki, import your deck, start learning

Everything you need to get the flashcards onto your devices. Install the app, import the file you downloaded, and start reviewing. If something does not behave, the questions at the bottom cover what comes up most.

What is Anki? A free program that makes remembering things easy. It uses active recall and spaced repetition, it handles pictures and audio, and it runs on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Windows and Android.

Anki is free on Mac, Windows, Linux and Android, and free in any browser through AnkiWeb. Paid versions and subscriptions exist, and none of them are needed for the decks in this shop.

Install Anki

On your Mac or PC
  1. Go to apps.ankiweb.net.
  2. Open the Download section, choose Mac or Windows, and click Download. On an Apple Silicon Mac, take the Apple Silicon build.
  3. Double-click the .dmg file you just downloaded.
  4. Drag Anki.app into your Applications folder. That is the install finished.
On your iPhone or iPad

The official AnkiMobile app costs $24.99 as a one-off purchase, direct from Anki. If you would rather not pay for it, there is a free app on the App Store that reads the same deck files.

  1. Open the App Store.
  2. Search for Anki APP-FlashCards Quizlet, or open the listing directly. The same app is also listed as SuperMCard.
  3. Tap Get to install it. It is free, on both iPhone and iPad.

These free apps are made by a third party, not by the Anki team. If you want the official app, search for AnkiMobile Flashcards instead. Either one imports the deck.

On your Android phone
  1. Open the Google Play Store and search for AnkiDroid Flashcards, or open the listing directly.
  2. Tap Install. AnkiDroid is free.

Import the flashcards

On your Mac or PC

Watch the walkthrough on YouTube, or follow the steps below.

The quick way. Double-click the .apkg file you downloaded and Anki opens it for you.

  1. In Finder, double-click the deck file.
  2. A window opens. Click Import.
  3. Anki confirms how many cards arrived.
  4. Click the deck and start reviewing.

If double-clicking does nothing. Import from inside the app instead.

  1. Open Anki from your Applications folder.
  2. Click Import.
  3. Select the .apkg file and click Open.
  4. Click Import to confirm, then pick the deck and start.
On your iPhone or Android

Watch the walkthrough on YouTube, or follow the steps below.

  1. Open the app and tap Retrieve Memory Bank.
  2. Tap Local File Import.
  3. Find the folder you saved the .apkg file to and tap the file.
  4. The import takes a few seconds. Go back to Home and tap Go Learn.

Common questions

Is Anki free to use?

Yes on Mac, Windows, Linux and Android, and free in any browser through AnkiWeb. The official iPhone and iPad app is a one-off purchase from Anki, and there is no subscription either way. You can sync between devices at no cost.

Do I need any special software?

Only the Anki app, which is available on Mac, Windows, iPhone, iPad and Android. Get it from apps.ankiweb.net or the App Store. Nothing else is required.

Will the deck work on both Mac and iPhone?

Yes. The .apkg format is universal. Import it on whichever device you use, and sync through a free AnkiWeb account to move between them.

Do I need an AnkiWeb account?

No, not to study offline. An account is free and optional, and worth setting up for backup and for using the deck across more than one device.

Can I edit the cards?

Yes. Add, delete or rewrite any card. You can also change the layout with Anki's card templates and add your own images or audio.

Can I print the flashcards?

No. The decks are built for the Anki app, which has no direct print option. The format is designed for spaced repetition on screen rather than on paper.

Google Drive says it cannot scan the file for viruses.

That is a warning, not an error, and it appears on any file too large for Drive to scan. Click Download anyway, then import the file as described above.

My browser says the file cannot be previewed.

Also expected. A .apkg file is a deck, not a document, so there is nothing to preview. Click Download and import it into Anki.
