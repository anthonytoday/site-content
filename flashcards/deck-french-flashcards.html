Do I have to pay for Anki?

Anki itself is free on Windows, macOS, Linux and Android, and free to use in any browser through AnkiWeb. Only the iPhone and iPad app is paid, and it is a one-off purchase from Anki, not from me.

Can I edit the cards?

Yes. Rewrite them, retag them, delete what you already know and add your own. Once the deck is imported it is an ordinary Anki deck and it is yours.
