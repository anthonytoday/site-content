Shop Help

Delivery, Refunds and Setup

One page covering both shops: what arrives after checkout, how to get it working, and what to do when something is wrong. The setup walkthroughs live on their own pages and are linked below.

Looking for setup instructions? They are split by product, because the two shops deliver differently.

Notion templates: how it works. Anki decks: how it works, or the full instructions with screenshots.

1

What Arrives After Checkout

Your link appears on the confirmation screen at checkout, and the same link arrives by email from an anthonytoday.com address. A Notion template arrives as a duplication link that adds it to your workspace. An Anki deck arrives as a download link for a single .apkg file. Keep that email: it is your proof of purchase and your route to future updates.

2

If the Email Did Not Arrive

Check your spam folder and, in Gmail, the Promotions tab, then search your inbox for anthonytoday.com. If it is still missing, write to me through the contact page with the email address you used at checkout, so I can find your order.

3

Refunds

14 days, no questions asked, on both shops. Reply to the delivery email and say the word.

4

Updates and Licensing

Updates are free on both shops. When a template or a deck is improved, I email the new link or file. Your existing copy, and for a deck your review history, is untouched, so you choose whether to move across. A Notion template may be shared inside your own organization. An Anki deck covers one person, so each member of a study group needs their own. Reselling or redistributing either one is not permitted.

Still Stuck

How do I reach a human?

Reply to your delivery email, or use the contact page. Include the order reference if you have it, which makes it much faster to find you.

Which shop is my order from?

Notion templates come from the template shop and open in Notion. Anki decks come from the flashcard shop and arrive as a file you import into Anki.
