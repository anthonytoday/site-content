Still stuck

How do I reach a human?

Reply to your delivery email, or use the contact page. Include the order reference if you have it, which makes it much faster to find you.

Which shop is my order from?

Notion templates come from the template shop and open in Notion. Anki decks come from the flashcard shop and arrive as a file you import into Anki. PLACEHOLDER: how the two confirmation emails differ.
