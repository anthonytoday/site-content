How to duplicate your template

1

Open your access link

Open the template from the direct link button, or from the link inside the PDF you received. The same link arrives by email from admin@anthonytoday.com. Keep that email: it is your proof of purchase and your route to future updates.

2

Click Duplicate

The template opens in Notion as a read-only preview. Press Duplicate or Start with Notion in the top right-hand corner of the screen.

3

Choose your space and add it

Notion asks where to put it. Select your space, then click Add to Private. If you belong to more than one workspace, pick the one you work in daily rather than a personal one you will abandon.

4

Wait a few seconds

The template appears under the selected workspace in your sidebar. Large templates with many databases take a little longer, so give it a moment before assuming something went wrong.

5

That is it. The template is yours forever

The copy is not shared with me, I cannot see it, and nothing syncs back. Rename it, delete half of it or restructure it entirely.

Start with the Read Me First section at the top of every template. It says which databases matter and what to fill in first. Ten minutes there saves an hour of guessing.
