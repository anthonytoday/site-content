TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE

How a spoken note on site becomes a closed, auditable action

Property teams do not lose work because people are careless. They lose it because the moment something is worth recording is the moment nobody can record it. This is the architecture built to close that gap, plane by plane.

THE PROBLEM

The people closest to the problem are the least able to record it

A leak, a late contractor, a tenant complaint: all of it is seen on site, by someone whose hands are full and whose laptop is somewhere else. The system of record sits at a desk. The information does not.

THE PAIN IT ADDRESSES

Work gets remembered instead of recorded, and the cost lands later

Issues resurface days later as a phone call, a message thread or a complaint. Nobody can say what is open per property, who was told, or when it was closed. Everything queues behind one person's inbox, and that person becomes the bottleneck.

THE SOLUTION

Push capture to the edge, keep judgement and approval in the centre

One tap turns speech into a structured record. AI triage decides what it is and where it belongs, a router hands it to the agent that handles that class of work, and nothing is marked done until a QA agent and a person have both signed it off.

EXHIBIT 1

The three plane architecture

The execution plane carries the work from left to right. The control plane sits above it and holds the two gates that decide whether anything closes. The data plane sits below it and holds the records every stage reads from and writes to.

PlaneWhat it doesWhat it is worth
Control A QA reviewer agent compares the output against the original spoken note, then a person approves or rejects it. Rejected work returns to stage 05. An audit trail per action, and a hard stop that keeps an AI mistake from being recorded as a finished job.
Execution Capture on the phone, ingest into one inbox, AI triage, routing, then three agents working the action in parallel. Time from noticing a problem to a logged, owned action drops to the length of a spoken sentence.
Data The inbox database, the property register, the vendor and tenant directory, and the closed record, all in Notion. One place to answer what is open, on which property, with whom, since when.
No typing on site 3 agents run at once Nothing closes without QA and a human One record per property and project

What it is worth, by who is using it

The person overseeing operations

Status across every property and project from one view, without chasing anyone for it. Work distributed to the right agent instead of piling into one inbox.

The person on site

An issue, a delay, or a status update gets reported the moment it happens. Hands stay free, and there is nothing to learn beyond talking into a phone.
