The pipeline, in five steps
01 Speak

iPhone Shortcut

A Back Tap or Lock Screen shortcut opens Wispr Flow and starts listening.

→
02 Land

Notion inbox

The transcript lands in one inbox database, timestamped and tagged to the property.

→
03 Read

AI triage

Notion AI and ChatGPT parse the transcript into what happened, where, and how urgent it is.

→
04 Act

Agent execution

Sub-agents run the action in parallel; a QA reviewer checks the work against the note.

→
05 Close

Tracked record

Status, owner and outcome sit on one page per property and, where relevant, per project.

Layer by layer

What each piece does, and the value it is built to produce

LayerWhat it doesBusiness value
Capture, iPhone Shortcut and Wispr FlowTurns a spoken note into a timestamped Notion page in one tap, no app switching, no typing.Removes the one barrier that kept the person closest to an issue from reporting it, so nothing is lost between the moment and the record.
Inbox, a single Notion databaseEvery voice note lands in one place, tagged to its property, before anything else happens to it.One system of record for the whole portfolio, not a mix of texts, calls and memory.
Triage, Notion AI and ChatGPTReads the raw, unpunctuated transcript and extracts the property, the urgency and the action required.Turns a rambling voice note into something a manager or an agent can act on immediately, without replaying the recording.
Execution, router and parallel sub-agentsAssigns each action to the specialist agent built for it, and runs several actions at once across different properties.Work moves without waiting on one person to sort a shared inbox; a maintenance dispatch and a tenant reply happen at the same time, not in sequence.
Oversight, QA reviewer and a human checkpointChecks every agent's output against the original note before status can move to Done, and escalates anything it is not confident about.Keeps a person in the decision loop, so speed never costs a wrong action reaching a tenant or a vendor.
Record, tracked property and project logEvery action, open or closed, sits on a page tied to its property and, where relevant, its project.One place to see status across every property and every active project, without asking anyone for an update.

What it is worth, by who is using it

The person overseeing operations

Status across every property and every active project from one view, without chasing anyone for it. A record of what was reported, by whom, and what happened next. Work distributed to the right agent instead of piling into one inbox to sort by hand.

The person on site

A way to speak up the moment something needs attention: an issue, a delay, a status update on the work in front of them, or a challenge blocking it. Hands stay free, and there is nothing to learn beyond talking into a phone.

The structural effect: adding a property or a project to the portfolio does not add a proportional amount of admin overhead, because the same four layers absorb it.
