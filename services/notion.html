I am not sure exactly what I need. Can you still help?

Yes. Most clients arrive knowing something is wrong without knowing the cause. If it is one specific problem, the 5-Day Diagnostic answers it with evidence and the fee comes off a build. If it is the whole workspace, the first days of the build work out what to fix first, before anything gets rebuilt.

How is this different from hiring an agency?

At an agency you talk to sales, then a project manager, then maybe the person doing the work. Here you work with one senior operator from the first call to the final handoff. The person who designs your system is the person who builds it.

I am not technical. Will I understand what you build?

That is the point. Every system ships documented, and I train your team so you can run and maintain it without me. You own it.

Most Notion consultants build workflows but never check who can see what. With a 15-year cybersecurity background, I build systems that are fast and safe, which matters most for client data, finance, and regulated teams.

What happens after the build?

You get full documentation, team training, and a system you own outright. If you want ongoing support as you grow, Notion Consultant as a Service keeps a certified consultant on call from $247 a month, but it is never required.

Why do you cost more than a freelancer?

Because the system works the first time. A cheaper build that breaks every month costs more in lost hours and rework than getting it right once.
