Questions

Before you start a conversation

I am not sure exactly what I need. Can you still help?

Yes. Most clients arrive knowing something is wrong without knowing the cause. That is what the audit is for: a focused review that tells you what to fix first, before you commit to a build.

How is this different from hiring an agency?

At an agency you talk to sales, then a project manager, then maybe the person doing the work. Here you work with one senior operator from the first call to the final handoff. The person who designs your system is the person who builds it.

I am not technical. Will I understand what you build?

That is the point. Every system ships documented, and your team is trained to run and maintain it without me. You own it.

Why does the security and governance angle matter?

Most Notion consultants build workflows but never check who can see what. With a 15-year cybersecurity background behind it, your system is fast and safe. That matters most for client data, finance, and regulated teams.

What happens after the build?

You get full documentation, team training, and a system you own outright. If you want ongoing support as you grow, Notion Consultant as a Service keeps a certified consultant on call from $247 a month, but it is never required.

Why does this cost more than a freelancer?

Because the system works the first time. A cheaper build that breaks every month costs more in lost hours and rework than getting it right once. You could stitch together templates and tutorials and hope they hold, or ask an AI to write your automations and babysit them forever. Or you build it properly, once.
