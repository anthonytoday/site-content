Questions

Before you reach out.

What does it cost?

A fixed price per engagement, quoted in writing after the first call once I have seen the workspace. An audit is the smallest way in; a full company workspace with automation and training is a larger project. No hourly billing.

I am not technical. Will I understand what you build?

That is the point. Every system ships documented, and I train your team so you can run and maintain it without me.

Do you work with individuals or only teams?

Both. Teams are where a full build pays off. For one person, an audit or a single working session is usually enough, and my templates cover the rest.

What happens after handover?

You own the workspace and the documentation. Ongoing support exists as a monthly retainer if you want it, and it is never a condition of the build.

Tell me what you need.

Describe the workspace and the problem. I reply within one business day and say plainly whether I am the right person for it.
