Questions

Before you start a conversation

I am not sure exactly what I need. Can you still help?

Yes. Most clients arrive knowing something is wrong without knowing the cause. That is the first thing the work establishes, before anything gets rebuilt.

How is this different from hiring an agency?

At an agency you talk to sales, then a project manager, then maybe the person doing the work. Here you work with one senior operator from the first conversation to the final handoff. The person who designs your system is the person who builds it.

I am not technical. Will I understand what you build?

That is the point. Every system ships documented, and your team is trained to run and maintain it without me. You own it.

Why does the security and governance angle matter?

Most Notion consultants build workflows but never check who can see what. With a 15-year cybersecurity background behind it, your system is fast and safe. That matters most for client data, finance and regulated teams.

Do you work inside our workspace?

Yes, at member level and without consuming one of your paid seats, as a registered Notion Consulting Partner. Access is scoped to what we agree and it is written down.

Why does this cost more than a freelancer?

Because the system works the first time. A cheaper build that breaks every month costs more in lost hours and rework than getting it right once.
