Questions

Before you start

I already started building. Is that wasted?

No. Half-built workspaces are the normal starting point. What you have gets assessed, and whatever is worth keeping is kept and connected properly rather than rebuilt for the sake of it.

What if I do not know exactly what I want?

That is what days one and two are for. You describe how the work moves, I decide the structure. Deciding what your business actually needs is the part you are paying for.

How is this different from a one-to-one Notion session?

A session is thirty minutes of being told what to build. This is ten days of it being built. The gap between those two is the reason most workspaces are never finished.

Can my team use it without training?

They get a recorded walkthrough and one live session. If someone still cannot run it after that, the thirty days of support covers it.

Do you need access to sensitive data?

No. The structure is built and tested on a handful of non-sensitive records. Confidentiality levels and access rules are set before anything sensitive moves in.

What if ten days is not enough for my business?

Then I tell you on the call rather than after you have paid. If your workspace is a 30-person operation with multiple entities, that is a different engagement and I will say so.

What happens when the thirty days of support run out?

Nothing breaks and you owe nothing. If you would rather not go back to having no one to ask, Notion Consultant as a Service keeps a certified consultant on call. Taken with the build it is $4,970 for both, which is $2,470 for the year against $2,970 on its own.
