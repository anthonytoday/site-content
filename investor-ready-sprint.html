You already know where this breaks

Not "you're disorganised." Every firm your size has these five. What they actually cost is the part nobody puts a number against.

  • An investor or lender asks for an update Days of principal time assembling something that should take an hour. Do that quarterly and it is a working fortnight a year, billed to nobody.
  • A diligence request lands The documents exist. Nobody can produce them on demand. A slow data room gets read as a signal about everything else you manage.
  • A date moves and nobody sees it Financing windows, option dates and lease breaks do not reschedule themselves. One missed date on one deal costs more than this entire engagement.
  • The person who knew where everything lived leaves Institutional memory walks out with them, and you rebuild it from old inboxes at your own hourly rate.
  • Operations are still manual when you raise or sell Diligence findings turn into price adjustments. A buyer pricing in key-person risk and undocumented process is repricing the firm, not a line item.

None of these are urgent on an ordinary Tuesday. They are only expensive on the day someone is looking at you, and that is never a day you get to choose.
